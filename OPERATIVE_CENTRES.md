**Function:** This is the source-of-truth intermediate written record for individual Operative Centre readings. The original corpus remains unchanged.  
**Method:** What changed? What made it sufficient? How does it remain achieved? The Centre is stated independently of any 54-cell coordinate.  
**Reading status:** “Written” denotes a completed editorial Centre reading; it does not mean a blind structural decoder run or proven canonical classification. “Needs further examination” denotes an unresolved reading.  
**Original organisation:** Part I — Original sentences (A-001–A-200); Part II — Fairy tales (27 tales); Part III — Mathematical traversals (615 claims and 4,871 steps). Parts II and III are reserved for progressive entries, not prefilled interpretations.

### A-001

**Source ID:** A-001  
**Original sentence:** The morning sun reaches mountain villages long before their valleys receive warmth.  
**What changed:** The receipt of morning sunlight and warmth becomes intelligibly differentiated across two connected locations.  
**What made it sufficient:** The temporal comparison establishes that villages receive the sun substantially earlier than their valleys receive warmth.  
**How it remains achieved:** An ordered temporal disparity remains established between the two locations.

**Diagnostic:** Removing “long before” eliminates the governing temporal precedence.
